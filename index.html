There Is No LSAT Magic: Here Is Exactly Where My -0 Broke

A strong section becomes easier to understand when you can see the decisions behind it.

The LSAT becomes manageable when you identify the repeatable job underneath each question. It's not about being "smart" enough to see the answer; it's about having a process to construct it.

When I took my last diagnostic before the real thing, I scored a -0 on Logical Reasoning. People often ask me what "clicked" to make that happen. The truth is, nothing clicked. There was no epiphany. Instead, there was just a rigorous, mechanical application of the rules we've discussed in this series.

The Process in Action

Let's walk through a full section under time pressure. Watch the decisions I make. Notice how I don't try to predict the right answer based on intuition. Instead, I prephrase functionally, I identify the structural gap, and I ruthlessly eliminate choices that don't match the routing instructions of the stem.
